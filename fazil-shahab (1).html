About

A Life Built on
Football & Service

Fazil Mohammad Shahab is one of Afghanistan's most distinguished football administrators, having served at the highest levels of the sport both nationally and regionally. As General Secretary and Finance Director of the Afghanistan Football Federation (AFF), he shaped the trajectory of Afghan football during a transformative era, working with FIFA, the Asian Football Confederation (AFC), and the Central Asian Football Association (CAFA) to grow the game in one of the world's most challenging environments.

His leadership was marked by a fierce commitment to inclusion — particularly the promotion of women's football in Afghanistan — at a time when doing so required courage as much as conviction. He oversaw the federation's finances, international relations, and its institutional compliance with FIFA standards, including finalizing and submitting the federation's 2021 audit report to FIFA.

When Afghanistan fell to the Taliban in August 2021, Shahab faced unimaginable personal danger. Forced to meet with Taliban authorities daily and provide information about players and staff, he nonetheless continued working — coordinating with FIFA President Gianni Infantino, international governments, and humanitarian organizations to evacuate hundreds of at-risk football players, officials, women athletes, and civilians to safety in Qatar, Albania, and the United States.

Today, Fazil Mohammad Shahab resides in Houston, Texas, continuing to advocate for Afghan communities abroad and lending his expertise to football development and humanitarian efforts.

Humanitarian Work

When Football Met
Courage & Crisis

⚠️

Under Taliban Pressure

After August 15, 2021, Shahab was in "grave danger" according to FIFA officials. Forced to meet with Taliban authorities daily and provide details about national team players, coaches, and staff — many of whom he was simultaneously trying to protect — he worked under extreme duress to buy time for evacuations to be arranged.

📱

Direct Liaison with FIFA

Served as a critical communications link between Afghan football players and FIFA President Gianni Infantino. He personally forwarded voice notes and evacuation documents from FIFA leadership to players via WhatsApp, coordinating visa letters, Canadian documentation, and departure instructions under life-threatening conditions.

✈️

Evacuation Coordination

Working in concert with FIFA, Qatar's government, and the Albanian authorities, Shahab helped facilitate evacuation flights for over 163 members of the Afghan football and basketball community. This included women's team players, male team officials, and their families — all at acute risk from the Taliban.

🏘️

Temporary Refuge in Qatar

Evacuees were initially housed in a FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 residential compound in Doha — a facility repurposed to serve as a safe haven. Shahab was himself evacuated and helped ensure the well-being of the community during this transitional period, which included COVID-19 testing, healthcare, and immigration support.

🇦🇱

Onward to Albania

By the end of 2021, the entire evacuated group had been relocated from Qatar to Albania under temporary protection status, where they received accommodation, meals, healthcare, language classes, and immigration support. Shahab was part of coordinating this second leg of the refugee journey.

🗽

Resettlement in the USA

Through sustained advocacy and coordination from 2021 to 2023, Shahab worked to secure permanent resettlement for Afghan evacuees in the United States and other third countries. Several former team members and officials were granted permanent resettlement in the U.S. and Canada, with Shahab himself ultimately settling in Houston, Texas.
